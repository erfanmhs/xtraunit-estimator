# Feedback round — 2026-09-10

Erfan's feedback from real use on his **iPhone**, with screenshots, after the
trade-package layer (PR #39) went live. Numbered so each item can be ticked
independently. Same standing rule as the last round: the phone is the design
target; the desktop must stay correct.

Status key: `[ ]` not started · `[~]` in progress · `[x]` done · `[>]` deferred
with a reason.

---

## 1. Takeoff viewer — the loupe is a lens

- [x] **The magnifier magnifies the spot under the fingertip; it should magnify
      the centre of the magnifier and select THAT point.**
      *Rebuilt as a true lens. The point that gets placed is the one under the
      centre of the loupe window on screen, and the loupe shows exactly that
      spot magnified. One function (`loupeLift`) positions the window and sets
      the aim point, so they cannot disagree. The lens sits 90 px above the
      finger; within 170 px of the top of the drawing it flips to 110 px
      below, and the aim point flips with it. The old "34 px above the finger"
      guess is gone.* Needs Erfan's thumb to confirm.

## 2. Project list — reordering

- [x] **Laggy and glitched; a card stayed floating over the others after the
      drag.**
      *Three causes. (a) Every drop saved and then revalidated the page, so the
      whole list re-rendered under the finger mid-edit — the save now writes
      the order without a page refresh. (b) Nine glass cards wiggling forever
      (backdrop blur re-rastered every frame) is what lagged — the wiggle is
      now three cycles, then a steady dashed ring. (c) A drag that ended
      without the card's own pointer-up (browser took the touch, capture lost,
      tab hidden) left the card pinned — a window-level safety net now ends
      the drag on any of those, and leaving edit mode lands every card.*

## 3. Sub quotes

- [x] **"No coverable lines in those divisions (already confirmed or
      excluded)" — the Union Plumbing quote could not be applied.**
      *Why it happened: the quote matched lines by CSI division only, and the
      only lines it could use were ones that were neither excluded nor already
      price-confirmed. In that project every plumbing line was one or the other
      (or there were none yet), so it refused — and said so in a way that did
      not tell you which.*
      *Now: a quote covers a **trade** (Plumbing, Framing… from the catalog;
      the AI reads the trade off the document). The message says exactly what
      is in the way ("Plumbing has 14 lines, but 14 already have a confirmed
      price"). A **"Replace confirmed prices"** tick spreads the quote over
      those too. And when the scope has **no** lines for that trade yet, the
      quote creates its own line under the trade, priced by the quote, with
      the sub's inclusions and exclusions carried over.*

## 4. Pricing — where does the total come from?

- [x] **Labor 3.25 + Material 0.25 + Equip 0.75 does not add up to $4,301.**
      *It is a rate per unit times the quantity: (3.25 + 0.25 + 0.75) = $4.25
      per sf × 1,012 sf = $4,301. On a laptop that sat in a tooltip; a phone
      has no hover. Every priced row now shows its arithmetic under the
      buckets: "1,012 sf × $4.25/sf = $4,301", or "Lump sum of the buckets =
      …", or "Entered total — overrides the buckets".*

## 5. Bottom bar — "Plans"

- [x] **The first tab says "Plans" but it opens the project page with the
      next steps too; call it something else, e.g. Project.**
      *Renamed to **Project** with a clipboard icon, everywhere the stage list
      is used (bottom bar, side rail, Go-to sheet).*

## 6. Takeoff — the flow is lost

- [x] **As soon as the takeoff opens, the app's flow disappears.**
      *The viewer hid the bottom tab bar on phones to give the drawing the
      whole screen. That is what broke the flow: no stages, no "where am I",
      no next step. The tab bar now stays on the viewer like every other page,
      and the viewer's own bar drops its Back / Go to (the tab bar does both).
      Cost: one strip of drawing height on a phone.* If that strip matters
      more than the flow on a real plan, say so and it can become a one-tap
      full-screen toggle instead.

## 7. Takeoff — two fingers mid-shape still placed a point (sixth report)

- [x] **After two single taps on an Area, the moment two fingers touched
      the screen to pan, a random point appeared and drew a triangle.**
      *Every earlier fix counted fingers with pointer events, in three places,
      and OR'd the counts; one missed finger and the first finger's aim stayed
      alive, the rubber band stood where the second finger touched, and the
      lift placed a point there. Replaced the mechanism: a document-level
      listener on native touch events reads the operating system's own finger
      count (`src/lib/takeoff/fingerCensus.ts`, tested). The instant it reads
      two, the aim is abandoned and the loupe and rubber band go; nothing is
      placed while two fingers are down; the remaining finger of a pinch
      cannot place when it lifts; and after every finger is off there is a
      300 ms cool-down before a fresh tap counts. Verified in the pane with
      synthetic touches: tap, tap, pinch (no point, no phantom), lift, tap
      inside the cool-down (ignored), fresh tap (placed).*
      *Second cause, found from the 7:29 am screenshot after #42 was live: no
      point was placed any more, but the dashed preview still ran off to a
      corner. That preview is the MOUSE hover (`setHover`), and a finger whose
      tap the census had cancelled kept sending moves that fell through to
      it — and nothing clears a mouse hover on a touch, so the shape stayed
      drawn after the lift. A finger now never sets the hover; its preview is
      the rubber band, which the census clears. Verified in the pane: a stray
      finger move leaves the preview at the draft's own points, a mouse move
      still adds the next segment.*

## 8. Takeoff — the nudge pad

- [x] **"I don't like this nudge menu, we don't need it. The user needs to
      learn to use the corner points and the magnifier."**
      *Gone on phones. A handle is adjusted by dragging it with the lens; hold
      a handle for Split / Delete point. The pad stays for a mouse, where it
      is small and there is no lens.*
